Going fractional feels like a risk.

Staying feels safe.

You have confused the two.

Your employer can restructure your role by Tuesday.
A fractional consultant cannot be made redundant.
They can lose a client.

That is a very different problem.

The people who built 4 clients before leaving their employer
never had to make a desperate leap.

They made a calculated trade — one income source
for four — and discovered they were never truly safe before.

Security was always in the portfolio. Not the payslip.
